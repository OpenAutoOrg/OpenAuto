# Case Study: Company A

A leading Danish construction retailer with a strong presence across the Nordics:
- Hundreds of stores across Denmark, Norway, and Sweden
- Serves both construction businesses and individual consumers
- Provides building materials, tools, and home improvement products
- Operates across multiple markets, each with its own local business needs

> *One business. Three markets. Different ways of working.*

---

## Business Challenges
Over time, each country and region has developed its own systems and manual processes to meet local needs. This has created several challenges:

- **Manual processes:** Create inefficiencies and require significant employee effort.
- **Inconsistent operations:** Different ways of working can lead to inconsistent store operations and customer experiences.
- **Delivery & fulfilment:** Challenges with orders and deliveries can affect efficiency and reliability.
- **Fragmented systems:** Different systems across countries make it difficult to scale improvements across the business.

*The challenges are interconnected, but their impact and urgency differ from one market to another.*

---

## Different market, different priorities
The most critical challenge is not necessarily the same in every country. For example:
- **Denmark:** Manual processes may create the greatest impact.
- **Norway:** Store operations may be a more urgent priority.
- **Sweden:** Delivery and order fulfilment may have greater business impact.

At the same time, Company A operates in a highly competitive market. Failing to address the most critical challenges could affect the company's ability to compete and serve its customers effectively.

*The question is: Where should Company A focus first?*

---

## One solution. Different realities
The executive board wants to invest in **one digital solution** that can deliver the greatest business impact. However, the solution cannot simply be rolled out in exactly the same way across all three countries. Your solution needs to consider:

- Local processes and business requirements
- Existing systems and technology
- Different operational needs
- Store and distribution operations
- The impact of change on employees and customers
- Different implementation and rollout approaches

> *One strategic solution does not necessarily mean one identical implementation.*

---

## Your mission as IT consultants
The company cannot solve every challenge at once. Your team, as IT consultants, needs to make the right strategic choices and focus on one opportunity that can realistically deliver meaningful impact to help Company A.

1. **01: Identify**
   Choose ONE key business opportunity to address.
2. **02: Prioritise**
   Explain why this opportunity should be prioritised over other challenges.
3. **03: Solve**
   Propose a digital solution and a focused MVP scope that demonstrates its potential business impact.
4. **04: Scale**
   Consider how the solution could be rolled out across different markets, taking local differences and implementation constraints into account.

> *Time to market is critical.*
> *Scope carefully. Focus on one problem. Maximise the impact.*
